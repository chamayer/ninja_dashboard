For association, intake, resolver and previews share the tiered matcher. It now
requires one eligible Computer and stops at ambiguity or a hard conflict; recency
does not choose or permit fallback. Serial and VM UUID matches also require
matching name or MAC evidence from a different, non-roaming source instance.
Intake, queued resolution, source-record conflict preview, reassessment, and the
one-time preview pass the source-instance identity into this shared decision.
Resolver promotion now evaluates each unmatched record separately; cluster peers
cannot lend a strong match or corroboration. Source identity is scoped to the
source instance, and promotion associates the exact observation rather than all
same-platform rows sharing an external key. Lookup-key candidates remain review
evidence only and do not attach records. Pure matcher/source-record tests and
14 opt-in PostgreSQL matcher tests pass, including cross-instance external-key
collision coverage. Migration 0382 applied in the disposable migration-chain
check; resolver promotion remains unverified in PostgreSQL.

Validation must cover both accepted positive and unknown/negative results, legacy
finding ownership, tenant restrictions, durable scheduling, and state changes. The
final design's acceptance table remains authoritative; this inventory does not
replace it or assert that the listed work has been implemented.
